# Postmark pitch: voiceover (George, ElevenLabs)

Everyone has a guess about where a place is.

Postmark turns that guess into a duel. You and a friend read the same clues, spin a real globe, and drop your pins. Nobody sees the other guess until both are locked. Then the destination is revealed, distance becomes points, and you bring home a stamp for your passport.

A game like this lives or dies on fair timing. Put every pin on Solana, and each guess is slow and costs a fee. Keep it all on a private server, and nobody can check the record.

So every Postmark duel runs on a MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollup. When your friend joins, the match is created on Solana and handed to a rollup. Each guess is sealed there instantly, and free for the player. A round can only be scored once both pins are locked. When the match ends, the final score is committed back to Solana, where anyone can check it.

Players never see a wallet or a fee. They see a match ledger, with a link for every step.

It's live today: twelve transactions a duel, all on MagicBlock.

Next comes a daily destination everyone plays, open leagues, and passports that travel with you.

Postmark. A world worth guessing.
